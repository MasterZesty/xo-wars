# 🎮 XO Wars: The Battle for the Grid

Welcome to **XO Wars**, a mythic reimagining of the timeless strategy game — Tic-Tac-Toe — now set in a cosmic battlefield of elemental warfare.

---

## 🌌 The Lore

In the ancient realm of **Gridaelia**, balance was maintained by two eternal forces: the **Xantrons** and the **Oryans**. These elemental beings governed opposing philosophies:

- 🟥 **The Xantrons (X)** – Masters of structure, symmetry, and offense. They believe in decisive, tactical strikes and calculated expansion.
- 🟦 **The Oryans (O)** – Guardians of flow, adaptation, and defense. They value harmony, fluid control, and surrounding rather than destroying.

For millennia, they coexisted peacefully on the **Sacred Grid** — a 3x3 battlefield suspended in the ether between time and space. But when the **Core Glyph**, the artifact that balanced strategy and serenity, was shattered... **war ignited**.

Now, each side seeks total control of the grid to restore their version of order.

---

## 🧱 Main Characters

### 🟥 General X – “The Blade of Logic”

- **Race**: Xantron  
- **Role**: Supreme Strategist  
- **Personality**: Calculating, stoic, brilliant tactician  
- **Weapon**: Twin Grid Blades that slice across dimensions  
- **Quote**: *“Symmetry is power. Control the center, and the game is yours.”*  
- **Backstory**: Born from the Axis Crystal at the grid’s origin point, General X rose through the ranks with unmatched precision. He believes emotions are a weakness and sees the war as a puzzle to be solved with perfection.

---

### 🟦 Commander O – “The Circle of Chaos”

- **Race**: Oryan  
- **Role**: Master of Flow  
- **Personality**: Playful yet cunning, adaptive, philosophical  
- **Weapon**: Orb Chakras that bounce unpredictably  
- **Quote**: *“The grid breathes, and I with it. Try to trap me—I'll wrap around you.”*  
- **Backstory**: Forged in the Spiral Wellspring, Commander O values unpredictability and flexibility. She believes that the grid must be danced upon, not dominated. She inspires loyalty through charm and chaos.

---

## ⚔️ The Grid Battles

Each match in **XO Wars** is not just a casual game — it's a **miniature war campaign**.

- The **center square** is the **throne**.
- The **corners** are strategic **outposts**.
- Every **line drawn** is a **battle line**.

Your moves represent the philosophies of your chosen side — logic or flow. The goal is not just to win, but to impose your version of balance upon the Sacred Grid.
